About

I am Ali Hussain Chowdhury Rahi — most people simply call me Rahi. I am a student, but more than that, I am someone who has chosen the web as a canvas. Design, to me, is not decoration; it is the quiet architecture behind every experience a person has online, and I have devoted the better part of my learning to understanding that architecture deeply.

Web design remains my sharpest and most passionate pursuit — the discipline where intention meets aesthetics, where a single decision about spacing or color can change how an entire interface feels. Alongside it, I have cultivated a wider range of applied skills: prompt engineering to communicate precisely with intelligent systems, video editing to shape raw footage into stories worth watching, image design through Canva to bring visual ideas to life, and content creation tailored for social platforms. None of these were learned in isolation; each was built and tested through real engagement within my professional network.

I approach every piece of work the same way — with patience, curiosity, and a refusal to settle for the obvious choice. That same mindset now drives a more ambitious pursuit: an original application, currently taking shape from the ground up, that I intend to design, build, and publish entirely on my own — from the first sketch to the Play Store and the App Store.
