Introduction

About Me

Web design is the craft I return to instinctively — the discipline where curiosity meets precision. I study composition, typography, and interaction with genuine fascination, and every project refines that instinct a touch further. It remains the pursuit I am most devoted to, and the one in which I have grown most swiftly.

Beyond design, I have cultivated tangible expertise in prompt engineering, video editing, Canva-driven visual design, and social media content creation. None of it was born in a classroom — it was forged through active networking within design and technology circles, honest feedback, and the discipline of shipping real work.

At present, I am conceiving and building an original application from the ground up, with an unwavering intention: to carry it from its earliest sketch to a polished, public release on the Play Store and App Store. This endeavour captures my process precisely — begin with a spark of an idea, refine it with deliberate care, and see it through to completion.
