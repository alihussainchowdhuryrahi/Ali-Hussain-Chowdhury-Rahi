About

Web design is where I do my best work. I study layout, typography, and interaction closely, and every project sharpens that instinct further — it's the skill I return to first, and the one I've grown fastest at.

I've also built real, working ability in prompt engineering, video editing, Canva-based image design, and social media content creation. None of it came from a classroom. It came from staying active in design and tech communities, taking real feedback, and shipping actual work.

Right now, I'm designing and building an original app from scratch — the goal is to carry it all the way from concept to a public release on the Play Store and App Store. It's the project that best shows how I work: start with an idea, refine it with intent, and follow it through to something finished.
